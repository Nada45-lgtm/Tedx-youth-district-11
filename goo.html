What is TEDx?

TEDx is a program of local, self-organized events that bring people together to share a TED-like experience. At a TEDx event, TED Talks video and live speakers combine to spark deep discussion and connection.

TEDx events are fully planned and coordinated independently, under a free license granted by TED. Our event is TEDxYouth@October11th, focused on youth voices, ideas worth spreading, and community impact.

To learn more, visit TEDx Official Website.
