About Us

TEDxYouth@October11th

At TEDxYouth@October11th, we don’t just share ideas — we ignite curiosity. The STEM team is where science, technology, engineering, and math come to life, turning complex concepts into exciting, hands-on experiences. We’re here to spark questions, challenge limits, and connect young minds with the thrill of discovery. Whether it’s through a scientific demo or an innovative workshop, we’re not just answering “what” — we’re diving into the “why” and “how,” helping every curious mind reach for the stars.
